LSAT Demon

My roleSenior Product Designer
Duration4 months · 2026

LSAT Demon

The work

I joined an established product and worked across several areas of the LSAT Demon experience, from admissions tools to features that help students study and understand their results.

I improved existing features and designed new ones within LSAT Demon’s design system, collaborating directly with the team and developers throughout the process.

What I designed

My work included the Scholarship Estimator, Roll Call, and ASK FAQ. I also explored new product ideas in Figma Make, including an AI-assisted Study Planner.

The project combined improvements to an established product with complex information design and new ways to help students study and make decisions.
